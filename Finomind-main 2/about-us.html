Our Story

Built for people living in two ledgers.

FinoMind started with a simple frustration: every advisor we spoke to understood one system, never both. One would optimise our Indian ITR and quietly ignore what it did to our Elster return. Another would sort our German taxes and have no idea what an NRE account was for.

We built the firm we were looking for — one that reads Form 67 and Anlage AUS in the same sitting, and treats DTAA as the starting point of a plan, not a footnote to it.

Today, FinoMind works exclusively with Indian professionals building a life in Germany, across six areas — from real estate to legacy planning — with one strategy connecting all of them.

One strategy, plotted across both systems — not two advisors working in parallel.
