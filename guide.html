Common Questions

Still stuck? Start here.

Can't find your question below — or want it answered for your exact situation? Send it to an advisor directly.

Do I still need to file an ITR in India if I live in Germany?+

Yes, if you have Indian-sourced income — rent, interest, or capital gains — regardless of your residential status. Your German income isn't taxed in India, but the Indian income still needs to be declared.

What exactly does DTAA prevent?+

It stops the same income from being taxed in full by both India and Germany. You claim credit for tax already paid in one country when filing in the other, using Form 67 or Anlage AUS depending on direction.

Can I keep my NRE account once I move back to India?+

No — once your residential status changes, an NRE account needs to be converted to a resident account or RFC account within a defined window. Keeping it open past that point isn't compliant.

Does my Steuerklasse affect how much tax I pay overall?+

It affects your monthly withholding, not your final liability — that's settled at year-end filing. The right class mainly changes your cash flow through the year, which matters if you're also managing money on the Indian side.

Is it worth investing in ELSS if I'm a German tax resident?+

It depends on how the gains are treated under both regimes and whether DTAA credit applies cleanly. This is one we'd want to look at against your specific portfolio, not answer in general terms.
